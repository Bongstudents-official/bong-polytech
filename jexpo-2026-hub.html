JEXPO 2026 Admission Portal & Information Hub

Your comprehensive guide to West Bengal Polytechnic Admissions, Online Tracking, and Merit Analysis.

📢 Important Notice Regarding JEXPO 2027 Exam Date: The traditional written OMR entrance examination has been officially discontinued by WBSCT&VE&SD. Admissions across all Government, Government-Sponsored, and Self-Financed polytechnics in West Bengal are now conducted directly via Class 10 (Madhyamik or equivalent) Merit List & Online Counselling on the Common Admission Portal (admission-tetsd.wb.gov.in).

🛠️ Official Eligibility Criteria (2027)

  • Citizenship & Domicile: Must be an Indian citizen. West Bengal domicile is mandatory for Government polytechnics and reserved quotas (open to all-India candidates in private institutes).
  • Academic Qualification: Passed (or appearing in) Class 10 / Madhyamik or equivalent examination from a recognized board.
  • Compulsory Subjects: Must have passed with Mathematics and Science / Physical Science (along with English as per board rules).
  • Age Limit: No upper age limit applies; candidates for 2027–28 are expected to be born on or before July 1, 2012 (subject to 2027 brochure).
