About Bong Polytechnic




Bong Polytechnic is a student-focused educational platform built to provide easy access to West Bengal Polytechnic previous year question papers (PYQ), diploma notes, and semester-wise study resources. Many students struggle to find WB Polytechnic 1st year question papers, 1st and 2nd semester PYQ, and subject-wise diploma papers because official websites are often unorganized and difficult to navigate. Our mission is to solve this problem by organizing all resources in a simple, fast, and student-friendly way.



On this website, you can find:



- WB Polytechnic 1st Semester Question Papers.
(Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry, Communication Skills In English)
- 2nd Semester Previous Year Question Papers PDF.
- Diploma 1st Year Study Materials and Notes.
- Subject-wise and year-wise organized PYQ.
- Quick access to important exam resources.
- All branches PYQs.



We focus on providing:



- Clean and mobile-friendly design
- Fast loading pages
- Easy navigation for all students


All materials are organized from publicly available sources, and proper credit is given to the Department of Technical Education, Government of West Bengal and those unofficial sites which are linked with our site.



Our goal is simple:



To help polytechnic students quickly find previous year question papers, prepare better for exams, and save time.


Bong Polytechnic is continuously growing to become a trusted platform for WB diploma students looking for question papers, notes, and semester-wise resources.
