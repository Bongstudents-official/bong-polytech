WB Polytechnic Admission 2027 – Eligibility, Fees, Documents & Counselling Guide

West Bengal Polytechnic admission 2027 (1st-year Diploma in Engineering & Technology, formerly JEXPO) is conducted by the West Bengal State Council of Technical & Vocational Education and Skill Development (WBSCT&VE&SD) for students seeking 3-year diploma courses after Class 10. Admission is merit-based rather than through a separate written entrance test, using Class 10 / Madhyamik marks as defined in the official information brochure. Based on the 2026–27 admission cycle, online applications for 2027–28 are expected to open around March–April 2027 on the official Common Admission Portal (admission-tetsd.wb.gov.in / sctvesd.wb.gov.in), followed by merit list publication and counselling in August–September 2027.

Eligibility for WB Polytechnic Admission 2027

Candidates must be Indian citizens who have passed (or are appearing in, subject to 2027 brochure deadlines) Class 10 / Madhyamik or an equivalent examination from a recognized board with Mathematics and Science / Physical Science. Following WBSCT&VE&SD's age rule, there is no upper age limit, while applicants for 2027–28 are expected to have a Date of Birth on or before July 1, 2012 (subject to confirmation in the 2027 brochure). Candidates must also meet standard physical and mental fitness norms (including no color blindness for applicable branches). Category reservations apply as per West Bengal government rules: SC (22%), ST (6%), OBC, EWS, Persons with Disabilities / PwD (5%), District Quota (20%, with a 25% female sub-quota), Land-Loser quota (2%), and supernumerary Tuition Fee Waiver (TFW, 5%) seats for eligible low-income families.

Documents Required

Keep original documents and self-attested photocopies ready for online upload and physical verification at the allotted polytechnic institute:

  • Class 10 / Madhyamik marksheet, admit card (as age proof), or passing certificate
  • Common Admission Portal registration confirmation slip, merit rank card, and seat allotment letter
  • Recent passport-size photographs and Aadhaar card
  • West Bengal domicile certificate (Proforma A, B, or C as applicable)
  • SC / ST / OBC / EWS / PwD / Land-Loser certificate (if claiming reservation)
  • Family annual income certificate (if applying under the TFW scheme)
  • Kanyashree ID (for female candidates claiming the Kanyashree fee concession)
  • Medical fitness certificate signed by a registered medical practitioner
  • Anti-Ragging Affidavit (as prescribed by the council at the time of admission)

WB Polytechnic Counselling Process

After registering on the Common Admission Portal (admission-tetsd.wb.gov.in) and paying the application fee, WBSCT&VE&SD publishes the state merit rank list based on Class 10 marks. Shortlisted candidates complete online choice filling and locking to select preferred government and self-financed polytechnics and branches in priority order. Seat allotment is released round by round (followed by spot counselling for vacant seats) based on merit rank, category/quota rules, and seat availability. Allotted candidates must pay the provisional/final seat booking fee online within the deadline, report for physical document verification, and choose either to freeze the allotted seat or opt for auto-upgradation in the next round.

Diploma Course Fees

In recent WBSCT&VE&SD admission brochures, the online application fee was ₹450 (with a 50% concession of ₹225 for valid Kanyashree enrollees). Tuition fees in Government polytechnic institutes are nominal at ₹50 per month (₹600/year, plus nominal admission, sessional, and caution money charges, or hostel rent/electricity fees where applicable), and tuition is waived for TFW seat holders. Self-financed / private polytechnics charge up to ₹1,500 per month for seats allotted through centralized counselling and ₹2,500 per month for management quota seats, excluding institute development, examination, caution deposit, or hostel fees. Exact 2027–28 figures will be confirmed in the official 2027 brochure.

Popular Diploma Branches

Popular 3-year diploma branches across West Bengal polytechnics include Computer Science & Technology (CST), Information Technology (IT), Mechanical Engineering, Civil Engineering, Electrical Engineering, Electronics & Telecommunication Engineering (ETCE), and Automobile Engineering, alongside specialized streams such as Architecture, Chemical Engineering, Metallurgical Engineering, Mining Engineering, and Food Processing Technology depending on the institute.
