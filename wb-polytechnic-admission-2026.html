WB Polytechnic Admission 2026 – Eligibility, Fees, Documents & Counselling Guide

West Bengal Polytechnic admission 2026 is one of the most searched topics among students who want to study diploma engineering after Class 10. Government, government-sponsored, and private polytechnic institutes across West Bengal offer diploma courses in Computer Science, Mechanical Engineering, Civil Engineering, Electrical Engineering, Electronics, and many other technical branches. Understanding the admission process, eligibility criteria, counselling rounds, fees, and required documents can help students make better career decisions.

Eligibility for WB Polytechnic Admission 2026

Students who have passed Class 10 from a recognized board can apply for diploma admission in West Bengal. Candidates should meet minimum marks criteria as announced in the official admission notification. Reserved category students may receive relaxation according to government rules.

Documents Required

Students should keep the following documents ready: Marksheet of Class 10, admit card, passport-size photographs, Aadhaar card, caste certificate (if applicable), domicile certificate, income certificate, and counselling application details.

WB Polytechnic Counselling Process

After registration, students select preferred colleges and diploma branches during counselling. Seat allotment is based on eligibility, merit, category reservation, and seat availability. Students can accept the allotted seat or participate in further rounds.

Diploma Course Fees

Government polytechnic institutes in West Bengal generally offer affordable fees compared with private institutions. Fees may vary depending on branch, institute type, hostel facilities, and admission category.

Popular Diploma Branches

Computer Science, Information Technology, Mechanical Engineering, Civil Engineering, Electrical Engineering, Electronics and Telecommunication, and Automobile Engineering remain among the most popular diploma branches in West Bengal.
